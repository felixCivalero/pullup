The Production Core6
03 — The core

The workers are the core.

When everyone decides alone

  • Content gets invented in a vacuum.
  • Marketing guesses what the work looks like.
  • Meetings multiply. Decisions don't.
  • The brand says one thing; the business does another.

When they decide together, every day

  • The daily work becomes the content.
  • Everyone can say who we are — and where we're going.
  • Thirty minutes is enough to decide.
  • Brand and business tell one story.
