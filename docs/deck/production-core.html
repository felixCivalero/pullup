03 — The core

The workers are the core.

When they pull in different directions

  • Content gets invented in a vacuum.
  • Marketing guesses what the work looks like.
  • Meetings multiply. Decisions don't.
  • The brand says one thing; the business does another.

When they align

  • The daily work becomes the content.
  • Everyone can say where we're going.
  • Thirty minutes is enough to decide.
  • Brand and business tell one story.

The 30-minute BKM flow

Two tracks. Thirty minutes each. One bridge.

Track B

Thirty minutes of the founder talking, shaped on the spot: Hook, Credibility, Core, Outro.

Bridge

Where A and B meet: the operational truth becomes line-by-line content the team can execute.
